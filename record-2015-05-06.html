Therapy session — May 06, 2015

Patient: Alex Turner   ·   Clinician: Dr. Sam Smith   ·   Record ID: AT-20150506
Session Overview - Presenting Issues Following his short-term goal plan, Alex reported going out to a bar on a Monday night to reduce his social anxiety. There he met a nurse named Lucia, and they chatted and played darts. Despite exchanging numbers, Alex felt too anxious to reach out to her. Key Themes and Discussion Points 1. Adjusting to Unfamiliar Environments ○ Alex has previously stated his inability to adjust to new environments. Forcing himself to go to a bar on Monday night allowed him to face his anxieties about feeling out of place. Alex reported having a good time despite some initial anxiety at the beginning of the night. 2. Sabotaging Relationships ○ Although Alex had a good time with Lucia, he did not reach out to her. His fear of rejection has manifested in a way that prevents Alex from forming meaningful relationships. He believes he will be rejected eventually, so he purposefully didn’t reach out as a way to avoid a negative outcome. Therapist Observations ● Dr.Smith observed that Alex has shown improving signs since last session by engaging in conversations with strangers and stepping out of his comfort zone by going to the bar on a Monday night ● Despite Alex’s improvement, his self-deprecating language has gotten worse indicating is lack of confidence needs improvement Goals and Action Plan ● Short-term: Take initiative to follow up on social plans once this week, like going out for drinks with coworkers. ● Long-term: Get comfortable with rejection in order to create long lasting relationships. Homework/Reflection: ● Reflect on moments where he feels like avoiding social situations. ● Practice one mindfulness exercise daily to manage stress and reduce feelings of isolation.
