Therapy session — November 04, 2014

Patient: Alex Turner   ·   Clinician: Dr. Sam Smith   ·   Record ID: AT-20141104
Session Overview - Presenting Issues Alex reported persistent feelings of stress and frustration related to his recent relocation to Chicago and the challenges of adjusting to a new environment. He shared that he often feels isolated and struggles to form new connections, leading to a sense of loneliness. Key Themes and Discussion Points 1. Adjustment to Relocation ○ Alex discussed the difficulties of adapting to his new surroundings, including adjusting to a different work culture and social dynamics. He expressed frustration with feeling "out of place” and uncertain about his decision to move. ○ Explored strategies for developing a sense of stability and comfort in his new environment. 2. Social Anxiety and Isolation ○ Alex described feelings of social anxiety, particularly in group settings, which has hindered his ability to make new friends. He mentioned that he avoids social events due to fear of rejection or judgment, and discussed past experiences with social anxiety and identified how it might be affecting his adjustment. Therapist Observations ● Dr. Smith noted that Alex appeared withdrawn and showed signs of low energy when discussing his difficulties in adapting to his new environment. ● Alex often used self-deprecating language, indicating a lack of confidence in his ability to adjust successfully. Therapeutic Interventions Used ● Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) - Cognitive Restructuring: Helped Alex identify and challenge negative self-perceptions about his social interactions. ● Social Skills Training: Practiced techniques for initiating conversations and building rapport with new acquaintances. ● Mindfulness Practices: Introduced exercises to help Alex stay present and reduce feelings of overwhelm in social situations. ● Solution-Focused Brief Therapy (SFBT): Worked on setting small, attainable goals for Alex to gradually increase his comfort in social settings. Goals and Action Plan ● Short-term goals: Attend at least one social event or activity in the coming week to practice the skills discussed in therapy. ● Long-term goals: Build a supportive social network in his new environment and enhance his confidence in social interactions. Homework/Reflection ● Reflect on moments when he feels socially anxious, noting any specific thoughts and re-evaluating them with more balanced perspectives. ● Practice one mindfulness exercise daily to manage stress and reduce feelings of isolation.
