Therapy session — October 04, 2017

Patient: Alex Turner   ·   Clinician: Dr. Sam Smith   ·   Record ID: AT-20171004
Session Overview - Presenting Issues Alex described his 5-day trip to California from September 28th to October 2nd. He states that he enjoyed it a lot, and put himself in a lot of situations where he was able to socialize with others and converse while having a good time. He took a lot of pictures. Once Alex gets back from California, he begins to drive back to his apartment from Midway Airport. He somehow ended up rear-ending Maya Caldwell on Lakeshore. They exchange numbers for insurance, the police come and file a report, and both Maya and Alex get checked out at St. Murky's. In the ER, a doctor examines Maya, and then Alex. After examination, Maya notices that Alex is in the next bed over, and they begin talking. She takes a quick nap after their conversation while waiting for the doctor. The doctor then examines Alex, relaying that he has no injuries. He is discharged. Alex also thought Maya was cute and decided to shoot his shot. He writes a note “Sorry ‘bout the accident, would love to make it up to ya ;)” with his phone number, and leaves the hospital. Key Themes and Discussion Points 1. Taking initiative to make plans with someone ○ Due to his social anxiety, Alex has had issues making plans to meet with someone. Leaving a sticky note expressing interest to meet up with Maya was a great way to get over his fear of rejection. 2. Experiencing unexpected situations ○ Alex has anxiety over new environments, but sometimes unexpected situations will happen that will force him out of his comfort zone. Alex did not mean to rear end Maya, but in doing so he had to face his social anxiety to resolve the situation. The trip Alex took helped with his anxiety which was part of the reason he handled the situation well. Therapist Observations ● Dr.Smith observed Alex’s calm reaction to the car accident. Alex has made improvements with his goal to be more care free. He was able to handle a stressful situation without being overwhelmed. ● Alex has made improvements with his social anxiety. He has expressed an interest in engaging in conversations with strangers like he did with Lucia. When given the chance to put himself out there, he took it by leaving the sticky note. Homework/Reflection ● Practice mindfulness exercises to cope with anxiety over the outcome of leaving the sticky note.
