About this collection

This is a simulated research portal created for an OSINT training challenge. Names, records, and events are part of the supplied fictional case file.

The archive supports keyword searching, browsing by year, and individual record pages. The source collection spans 2014–2025.

Research tip: events mentioned in one session may be followed up in later entries. Dates and names are often more useful than broad keywords.
