Therapy session — March 19, 2025

Patient: Alex Turner   ·   Clinician: Dr. Sam Smith   ·   Record ID: AT-20250319
Session Overview - Presenting Issues Alex is flabbergasted when he comes into his therapy session. Alex states how Lucia just told him that she was pregnant with twins and that she had already gone to an OB appointment to confirm. He said he was speechless, and felt that at the bottom of his heart, he was not really happy about this. He states that he finally began to have some consistency in his life, and that kids were not in the picture for another year or two. He also stated that even though this was taken with many surprises, he always prefers things in even numbers, no matter if it is children, food, miles, etc. He thinks that singular objects are very much bad luck. Therapist Observations ● Alex is experiencing anxiety over something unexpected again. His fear of change is manifesting again since he feels unhappy about the surprise pregnancy. Homework/Reflection ● Be considerate of Lucia’s feelings. This situation is something shared between Lucia and Alex. Be honest with her, but be mindful of how words can come across to her while she’s in this vulnerable position. ● When feeling anxious and depressed, focus on what is in his control. Unexpected things are out of his control, but his reaction to it is within his control. Practice this mindful exercise to manage anxious thoughts.
